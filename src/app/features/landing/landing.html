How a run works

You can see every step, and you make the call

  1. You set the goalWrite a brief, pick a campaign or ask for matches.
  2. The agent works in the openEach step appears live as it reads and assesses.
  3. It shows its evidenceQuotes come from the source text, and limits are stated.
  4. It stops and asks youEdit the proposal, approve it or turn it down.
  5. It acts and reports backOnly what you approved is carried out.

Built-in limits

Helpful agents, firm limits

  • No approval, no change. Agents can only propose until you approve.
  • Your permissions still apply. Agents act as you, and can't reach what you can't.
  • A local model. Qwen runs on your own machine, with no cloud model behind it.
  • Applicant text stays data. Attempts to instruct the AI are flagged, not obeyed.
